Where kitchen water comes from and where it goes

Most kitchen leaks start under the sink. A supply stop can weep, a disposal flange can loosen, or a drain connection can slip, and the water lands on the cabinet floor. From there it spreads sideways into the toe-kick cavity, down through the pipe cutouts and into the subfloor. In a house with a basement or crawl space below, it may drip into the ceiling underneath without a trace upstairs.

The U.S. Census Bureau counts 16,981 households in Draper, averaging 2.87 people, which means most kitchens work hard every day. It also lists the median owner-occupied home value at about $785,000, so cabinets and flooring are often a large share of the loss. Leaks tied to the dishwasher, fridge or washer are covered on the appliance leak page.

Kitchen leak safety steps for the first hour

Close the sink stop

Turn the small valves under the sink clockwise. If they will not seal, close the main water valve.

Keep electricity away

Do not use outlets near the water or run the disposal. If water reached an outlet or the panel, call an electrician.

Empty the cabinet

Remove cleaners, pots and stored items so the base can be inspected and dried.

Call and photograph

Call (385) 988-9410 for water removal. Photograph the leak, the cabinet and the floor before anything is changed.

A wet floor around the stove or fridge can be slippery, so wear shoes. Keep children out of the room.

Detach, dry and reset the cabinet run

1

Extract and map

Portable extraction units remove standing water. Moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera find wet cabinets, walls and subfloor.

2

Open the cavities

Toe-kick boards come off and small holes may be made in cabinet backs so air can reach the wet areas.

3

Detach if needed

When the countertop or a cabinet blocks drying, the run may be detached and set aside, then reset after drying.

4

Dry the structure

Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers dry cabinets, walls and the floor, with readings logged at each visit.

5

Clean and treat

Surfaces get antimicrobial treatment where needed, and we list the interior repair scope.

Solid wood cabinets often dry and stay. Particleboard boxes that swelled usually do not, so they are replaced. Tile and laminate over a wet subfloor may need to come up. ANSI/IICRC S500 is a reference many professionals work from, and EPA guidance says to dry wet areas within 24 to 48 hours.

Pricing factors for a kitchen water job

Cabinet material

Solid wood and particleboard behave very differently when wet.

Countertop type

Stone and laminate tops affect whether a detach is needed.

Flooring

Tile, laminate and hardwood each dry on different terms.

Water reach

Water reaching the floor below doubles the drying area.

Leak duration

A day of dripping is not the same as weeks.

Kitchen layout

Island bases and tight corners limit access.

Insurance after a sink or supply leak

Insurers distinguish sudden damage from long-term seepage, and a drip that ran for weeks can be treated differently from a burst fitting. Ask what your policy says about cabinets, flooring and countertops, and whether the failed part is included. We cannot promise coverage. Keep the replaced part and photographs, since adjusters like to see them.

Permits, plumbers and replacing cabinets

Replacing a leaking fitting is plumbing work and belongs to a plumber. Draper City says its online portal takes plumbing and remodel or alteration permit applications, so ask the city or your contractor what a cabinet or floor rebuild needs before you start. We dry and clean, and then you can bring in whoever repairs the kitchen.

Habits that cut down on sink base surprises

  • Look under the sink monthly for stains, drips and a musty smell.
  • Replace brittle supply lines before they fail.
  • Keep a shallow tray or liner in the cabinet to catch drips.
  • Do not store cleaners where they can hide a slow leak.

A quick monthly look catches the small leaks that cause the biggest cabinet losses.

Common questions

Yes, you can reach dispatch at (385) 988-9410 at any hour. Same-day response is available when a crew is available, though we never promise a clock time.

Can wet cabinets be saved?

Solid wood often can. Swollen particleboard usually cannot, and moisture readings help decide.

Do you remove the countertop?

Only when it blocks drying. The run may be detached, dried and reset afterward.

What about water that went through to the basement?

We check the level below too, because water that passes through a floor leaves both areas wet.

Do you fix the leaking pipe?

No. A plumber repairs the source. We remove the water, dry the kitchen and handle the interior repair scope.
