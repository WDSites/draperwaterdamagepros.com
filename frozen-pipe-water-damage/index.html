Why Draper pipes freeze and split at the thaw

Water expands as it turns to ice. A frozen pipe often splits along its length, but the ice plug holds the water back, so nothing leaks while it stays frozen. The flood begins when the plug melts and pressure pushes water through the crack. People often find the damage on a warm afternoon, long after the cold night that caused it.

Salt Lake Valley winters give pipes plenty of chances. The National Weather Service's Salt Lake City normals list January as the coldest month, with an average low near 24 degrees. The same normals show about 21 days a year when the high never climbs above 32, so pipes in a poorly heated space can stay frozen all day. If the pipe has already failed under pressure, the burst pipe page covers that side.

Frozen pipe safety steps before and after the thaw

Shut off the water

If a pipe is frozen but not yet leaking, close the main valve and open the faucet on that line. If it is already leaking, close the main first.

Thaw with gentle heat

Use a hair dryer or warm towels. Never use an open flame, torch or propane heater.

Watch the electricity

Keep clear of outlets and the panel if water is running nearby.

Call and document

Call (385) 988-9410 for water removal and a plumber for the pipe. Photograph frost, splits and wet surfaces before anything gets moved.

If you cannot find the frozen section, do not keep heating random walls. Leave the water off and let a plumber locate it.

Insurance and a vacant house in winter

Freeze claims often hinge on whether heat was kept on. Many policies expect an empty home to stay heated, so read yours before you travel in winter. Ask your insurer what it requires for a vacant home and what proof of heating they want. We cannot promise coverage, but dated photos and drying records show the adjuster what happened.

Preparing before the next cold snap

  • Disconnect hoses and drain or shut off outdoor spigots before freezing nights.
  • Open cabinet doors on exterior walls so room heat reaches the pipes.
  • Let a faucet drip on the coldest nights when a line has frozen before.
  • Keep the thermostat set above the lowest setting when you travel.
  • Know where your main shutoff is and make sure it turns.

Open garage doors and gaps where pipes enter a wall are common culprits.

Frost depth, buried lines and the local picture

NOAA's state climate summary says average winter lows are below freezing across Utah, so cold is normal here. Common questions

Is emergency frozen pipe water damage restoration available in Draper?

Yes. Our line at (385) 988-9410 is staffed around the clock, and a crew can come the same day when one is available. We do not name an arrival time in advance.

Can I thaw a frozen pipe myself?

A hair dryer or warm towels is a safe way to try. Never use a flame or torch, and call a plumber if you cannot reach the frozen section.

Why did my pipe leak only after it warmed up?

The ice acted like a plug. Once it melted, water flowed through the crack that the freeze had already made.

Will insurance cover freeze damage?

That depends on your policy and on whether heat was maintained. Ask your insurer before you travel, and keep photos and records.

Do you fix the split pipe?

No. A plumber repairs the pipe. We remove the water, dry the structure and handle cleanup and the interior repair scope.
