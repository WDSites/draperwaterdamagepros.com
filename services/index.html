Cleanup and repair come after drying. Water damage cleanup covers removing ruined materials and sanitizing, and water damage repair covers the rebuild. Storm, groundwater and sewage events are on flood damage restoration.

How the stages fit together

Most losses touch three or four of these services in order. A supply line fails on a Saturday night, a crew pulls out the standing water, and drying equipment then runs for several days while the readings fall. Once the structure is dry, ruined drywall and flooring are replaced. Each stage ends with a measurement, not a guess.

  • Standing water or a spreading leak: start with emergency water removal.
  • Wet walls, floors or ceilings with no puddle: mitigation and drying.
  • Materials already torn out, stained or smelly: cleanup, then repair.
  • Storm runoff, groundwater or a sewer backup: flood damage restoration.
  • Musty smell or visible growth after a leak: mold remediation.

Water from a clean supply line is handled differently than water from a drain. The service areas page lists the nearby towns we cover, and the about page explains how a call is handled.
