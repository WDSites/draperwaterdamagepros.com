Where commercial water losses start and how far they travel

The U.S. Census Bureau estimates Draper's population at 50,652 as of July 2025, and the buildings that serve those residents range from single suites to larger multi-tenant properties. Water losses in them tend to start in the same few places. A supply line fails above a ceiling, a sprinkler head discharges, a roof or wall leaks, or a drain backs up.

Size changes the problem. Water that would stay in one room of a house can run across an open floor plate, drop through ceiling tile, and reach the suite next door through a shared wall. A commercial loss is often a question of how many rooms, not how deep the water is.

We handle the water side of that loss: extraction, drying, cleanup and the interior repair scope. Plumbers, fire sprinkler contractors and roofers fix whatever caused it.

Safety steps for a business building in the first hour

Find the shutoff

Close the supply valve for the suite or the building if you can reach it. Leave sprinkler system valves to the fire protection contractor or the fire department.

Treat power with care

Keep people out of rooms where water reached outlets, panels or floor boxes. Move computers and equipment up and away from wet floors.

Call the people who decide

Notify the property manager or owner, your insurer, and (385) 988-9410 for extraction and drying. A plumber or contractor handles the source.

Document before moving

Photograph the source, the ceiling and the floor, then list damaged inventory. Move stock and files to dry areas.

After-hours calls are normal for this work, and dispatch runs 24/7. For standing water, see our page on emergency water removal.

How we dry and clean a larger floor plan

1

Scope the loss

A technician walks every affected room, using moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera to find wet areas behind finishes.

2

Extract standing water

Submersible pumps, portable extractors and truck-mounted units remove water from floors and low spots.

3

Remove what cannot dry

Wet ceiling tile, insulation, carpet pad and saturated drywall come out so cavities can dry.

4

Dry in zones

Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers are set up room by room, and the zones are closed so readings can be tracked.

5

Clean and prepare for repairs

Surfaces get antimicrobial treatment and the interior repair scope is prepared, as described under water mitigation.

ANSI/IICRC S500 is a reference many professionals work from when planning drying. Daily readings go in a log that the building owner can share with an adjuster.

What changes the price of a business water job

Floor area

A larger affected area needs more extraction time and more equipment.

Number of rooms and suites

Each separate space needs its own readings and drying zone.

Finish materials

Ceiling tile, carpet tile, drywall and cabinetry each have a different removal and drying scope.

Water category

Clean supply water is simpler than a drain backup or a contaminated release.

Access and timing

Locked suites, shared access and after-hours work affect scheduling and labor.

Drying length

Concrete floors and dense materials extend the number of equipment days.

Scope and pricing are explained before work begins, and we do not quote a figure before we have seen the building.

Owner, tenant and insurer: who handles what

In a leased space, the lease and the policy decide who is responsible for the structure, the improvements and the contents. Ask your property manager and your insurer which policy responds, and whether it wants a particular contractor or documentation. Sudden discharge and gradual leaks are treated differently by most carriers. We cannot promise any outcome, but photos, moisture logs and an itemized scope make the conversation easier.

Draper City licensing and permits for the repair stage

Draper City's online portal takes applications for Commercial business licenses, along with Plumbing, Mechanical and Remodel/Alteration permits. If rebuilding after a loss changes piping, ductwork or the layout, ask the building owner or contractor whether a permit applies. We handle the drying and cleanup, and the licensed trades handle their own permitted work.
