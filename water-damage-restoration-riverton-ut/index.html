How the Riverton water year plays out

Water trouble in Riverton follows the calendar more than most places.

Spring runoff and storm season

Riverton's stormwater division lists storm season as April through October. Salt Lake City's wettest month is April (that is the airport station, not Riverton itself). Spring water tends to arrive at foundations, window wells and basement walkouts, and a wet basement wall can soak carpet and drywall before anyone notices.

The city offers sandbags, 25 per household, so grab them before a forecast storm instead of during one. Sandbags slow surface water but will not stop a failed sump pump.

Irrigation season

Riverton runs its own secondary irrigation system, fed by canal shares and Jordan River rights. When it is switched on for the season, a sprinkler line break or a stuck zone can put water against a foundation for days. Secondary water is untreated, so water from that source that enters a home should be treated with caution, not mopped up casually.

The first fall freeze

At the first hard freeze, water left in a hose bib, an exterior line or a garage supply pipe can freeze and split. The leak often shows up only after the thaw, inside a wall or a ceiling. Salt Lake City's January mean low is about 24 degrees, so cold snaps do reach pipe temperatures here.

Monsoon season

On August 3, 2023, monsoon storms hit the south valley and the neighboring city of Draper recorded the highest rainfall total. The report did not name Riverton, so treat it as regional context. Even so, a short heavy storm can overwhelm a roof or window well fast.

When water is spreading in Riverton

Work through these in order, and stop at any step that feels unsafe.

  1. Shut off the source. Close the fixture valve or the main valve for a plumbing failure.
  2. If there is any sign of electrical or gas risk, leave and call the utility or 911 first.
  3. Turn off power to wet areas at the panel only if you can reach it without standing in water.
  4. Lift small valuables, rugs and electronics onto dry surfaces.
  5. Keep people and pets out of water that may be contaminated, especially sewer backup or water that came in from outside.

Photos before you move things

Take quick photos and video of the damage for your insurer. Do it before cleanup, but do not wait for it if water is still rising.

Find your main shutoff

If water is still spreading, locate the main valve and turn it off.

Clear a path

Move furniture away from wet walls and floors so crews can get extraction units in quickly.

Do not run the HVAC

Leave the furnace and fans off if the water may be contaminated, since air handling can spread it. We will tell you what to do on the call.

Services, in the order Riverton homes tend to need them

Most calls start with standing water, so emergency water removal comes first: extraction, then a quick check of what the water reached. Next comes drying. Water mitigation covers the meters, air movers and dehumidifiers that stop wet drywall and framing from staying wet.

After the structure is dry, water damage repair rebuilds what had to come out. When the water was a storm surge into a lower level, flood damage restoration applies. Our full list is on the services page.

Wet material left for more than 24 to 48 hours risks mold, per the EPA. If you see growth, mold remediation follows ANSI/IICRC S520, and the key is fixing the moisture source.

Who handles what in Riverton

  • Culinary water: Riverton City runs it and has bought supply from Jordan Valley Water Conservancy District since 2015.
  • Secondary irrigation: city-run, fed by canals, and separate from the culinary system.
  • Sewer: Jordan Basin Improvement District.
  • Storm drains and sandbags: Riverton Public Works Stormwater Division.
  • Permits: Riverton City Building Department. Ask them whether your repair needs one.

Our crews deal with water inside your home. The utility deals with the pipe outside it.

What shapes the cost of a Riverton job

Water category

Clean, gray and black water (S500 vocabulary) need different handling, and contaminated water takes more labor.

Area and materials

A wet carpet is a smaller job than wet drywall, insulation and subfloor across several rooms.

Drying time

Equipment count and days on site depend on how deeply the water soaked in.

On insurance, sudden and accidental water damage is often covered by homeowners policies, while gradual leaks and neglect often are not. Flood is usually excluded and needs separate flood coverage. Check your policy and ask your insurer what applies to you.

Riverton, the Jordan River and 84065

Riverton takes its name from the Jordan River, and canals once carried river water to the benchland. The South Jordan Canal and the Utah and Salt Lake Canal are still in use.

A September 2005 thunderstorm flooded some Riverton and South Jordan basements. Most homes here were built from the late 1960s onward, so many have lower levels that gather water when drainage is overwhelmed.

Does Riverton's secondary water cause water damage?

It can. A broken sprinkler line or valve box leak sends untreated water toward foundations. Shut off the zone, then call so we can check for wet walls and subfloors.
