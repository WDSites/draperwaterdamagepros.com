How a burst supply line floods a Draper home

A supply line carries water under pressure all day. When a fitting separates or a pipe splits, the water does not trickle. It sprays or pours until someone closes a valve. A drain leak is different, because drain pipes only handle what you send down them.

In a two-level or basement home, gravity does the rest. Water from an upstairs bathroom follows framing and wiring holes, then shows up far from the break. Draper City's utilities page says the city owns and operates one of the two water systems serving residents, and WaterPro/Draper Irrigation runs the other, so your meter may belong to either one. If ice caused the break, see the frozen pipe page.

Burst pipe safety steps for the first hour

Shut off the water

Close the main valve, usually where the line enters the house near the meter or water heater. Then open a low faucet to drain the pipes.

Mind the electricity

Stay away from wet outlets, light fixtures and the panel. Use the panel only if it is dry.

Call the right people

Call (385) 988-9410 for water removal. A plumber repairs the pipe, and the water utility's after-hours line can help if the main valve will not turn.

Protect and document

Lift electronics out of the wet area and photograph the break and the damage first.

Do not stand beneath a bulging ceiling. If you smell gas or see sparks, leave and call 911 from outside.

Our extraction and drying work after a supply line break

1

Map the water

Moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera show where water traveled through framing, insulation and subfloor that looks dry on the surface.

2

Extract

Submersible pumps clear deep water. Portable and truck-mounted extraction units then pull water out of carpet, pad and flooring.

3

Open what is wet

Soaked insulation comes out. Where a wall cavity cannot dry in place, we make a flood cut, a straight cut through the drywall just above the wet line.

4

Dry the structure

Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers run in a closed space while we log readings at each visit.

5

Clean and treat

Surfaces get antimicrobial treatment where needed, and we write up the interior repair scope.

ANSI/IICRC S500 is a reference many professionals work from, and EPA guidance says wet areas should be dried within 24 to 48 hours. Equipment comes off once the structure reads close to an unaffected room, as our structural drying page explains.

Insurance questions after a sudden pipe failure

Insurers tend to separate sudden, accidental discharge from damage that built up slowly, and a burst supply line is normally described as sudden. Flood coverage is a different matter, since flood means water entering from outside the home. Ask your insurer how your policy treats plumbing discharge, whether the failed pipe is included, and what records they want. We cannot promise coverage, but photos and moisture readings give you a clear record.

Mistakes that make a burst pipe worse

  • Waiting to see whether the flow slows down. Line pressure does not ease on its own.
  • Turning the water back on before a plumber has repaired the line.
  • Closing up or painting over a wall that has not been checked for wet insulation.
  • Throwing out damaged items before photographing them.

Utility lines, permits and who handles each part

Draper City says its water division maintains water lines, valves and fire hydrants. If water comes from the street side or the meter box, call the utility, which keeps an after-hours line.

Inside the house, a plumber repairs the pipe. Draper City's permit portal accepts plumbing and remodel or alteration applications, and your plumber or contractor will know which one fits a re-pipe or a rebuilt wall. We also serve nearby towns, including Riverton and Alpine.

Common questions

Is emergency burst pipe water damage cleanup available in Draper?

Yes. Dispatch runs 24/7 on (385) 988-9410, and same-day response is available when a crew is available. We do not promise an arrival time, but we tell you what to do while you wait.

Should I shut off the water at the meter or inside the house?

Use the main valve inside the house first, since it is the quickest. If it will not turn, the water utility's after-hours line can help with the meter.

Will my walls have to be cut open?

Sometimes. Moisture readings decide, and a flood cut is made only where a cavity is wet and cannot be dried in place.

How long will drying take after a burst pipe?

It depends on how far the water spread and what it soaked, so we do not quote a number up front. Daily readings show when the structure matches an unaffected area.

Do you repair the broken pipe?

No. A plumber repairs or replaces the pipe. We extract the water, dry the structure and handle the cleanup and interior repair scope.
